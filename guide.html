1. What Are PVC Shoe Charms?

PVC shoe charms — also described generically as clog shoe charms or shoe pins — are decorative accessories designed for perforated clog shoes. Brand names used by footwear companies may be protected trademarks; SHANXING is an independent manufacturer and is not affiliated with those brands.

Soft PVC is suitable for bright colors, black outlines and layered designs. A standard backing is assembled on the reverse so the finished charm can be inserted into compatible shoe holes.

We manufacture customer-owned or properly authorised artwork only. Club logos, tournament marks, characters and third-party brand assets should be cleared by the buyer before production.

3. How PVC Shoe Charms Are Made

Understanding the process helps you interpret quotes and lead times. The standard method is drip molding (滴胶工艺):

  1. Mold manufacturing. After artwork approval, a specialist mold-making partner manufactures the mold. Typical mold-making time is 2–3 days, subject to design complexity.
  2. PVC preparation. Soft-PVC compound is color-matched to the approved reference. Material choices and report coverage are reviewed against the buyer's intended market.
  3. Layer-by-layer injection. Liquid PVC is dripped or injected into the mold cavity, one color layer at a time. Each layer is partially cured before the next color is added.
  4. Heat curing. The filled mold is heat-cured so the color layers form the soft-PVC face.
  5. Demolding & backing assembly. Cured charms are removed and assembled with the selected shoe-charm backing.
  6. QC & packaging. Appearance, dimensions and backing assembly are checked before packing.

💡 Production responsibility: mold manufacturing is handled by specialist partners, while shoe-charm production stays in our Fujian factory. Repeat-order mold charges depend on mold condition, design changes and the terms confirmed in your quotation.

What drives the per-unit cost?

  • Volume — larger production runs generally reduce the budgetary unit price.
  • Design complexity — more colors, raised layers and finer details can slow production and change tooling requirements.
  • Size — larger charms use more material. Standard is 2.5–4cm.
  • Backing type — plastic pin (standard) vs metal rivet (premium).

SHANXING's standard starting MOQ is 100 pcs per design. The exact minimum and tier depend on the approved artwork and production assumptions.
