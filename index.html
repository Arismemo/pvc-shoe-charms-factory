Buyer FAQ

Everything you're about to ask

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our MOQ starts at just 100 pieces per design — among the lowest in the industry. Ideal for small brands, Etsy sellers, and Amazon FBA sellers testing new designs. Larger orders (5000+ pcs) unlock our lowest pricing tier at $0.12/pc and include free mold.

How much do custom PVC shoe charms cost in bulk?

Volume-tiered pricing: 100–499 pcs at $0.50/pc, 500–1999 pcs at $0.30/pc, 2000–4999 pcs at $0.18/pc, 5000+ pcs at $0.12/pc. Mold fees range $30–$80 by design complexity, waived on orders above 5000 pcs. Send your artwork for an exact quote within 24 hours.

How long does sampling and production take?

Sampling: 3 business days after artwork is received. Bulk production: 7–10 days for under 5000 pcs, 12–15 days for 5000–50000 pcs. We arrange both air freight (5–7 days) and sea shipping (25–35 days) worldwide.

Do your PVC shoe charms meet EU and US safety standards?

Yes. Our PVC is phthalate-free and complies with EN71 (EU), CPSIA (US) and REACH. Test reports available on request — essential for children's products and Amazon compliance.

Can I order custom shapes, colors and 3D designs?

Absolutely. We support 2D flat, 3D sculpted, and multi-layer designs in any Pantone color. Send a vector file (AI/EPS/PDF) or even a hand sketch — our design team returns a digital proof within 24 hours, free of charge.
