Buyer FAQ

Everything you're about to ask

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard starting MOQ is 100 pieces per design. The exact minimum depends on artwork complexity, size, colors, backing and packing.

How much do custom PVC shoe charms cost in bulk?

For the standard assumptions listed above, budgetary EXW pricing is $0.30/pc for 100–499 pcs, $0.20/pc for 500–1999 pcs, $0.15/pc for 2000–4999 pcs, and from $0.10/pc for qualifying 5000+ pc orders. Send the artwork for an exact quote.

How do payment and sample fees work?

Molds and samples are paid before work starts, and the sample fee is credited against your bulk order. For bulk orders, a 50% deposit starts production and the balance is due before shipment.

How long does sampling and production take?

Sample production starts from 3 working days after artwork and mold details are confirmed. Complex designs may take longer, and international shipping time is excluded. After sample approval, bulk production takes about 7–10 days for under 5,000 pieces and 12–15 days for 5,000–50,000 pieces.

Do your PVC shoe charms meet EU and US safety standards?

Supplier material test reports are available on request. We first check which material, standard and test items the report covers. Finished-product or batch testing may still be required depending on the destination market, product use and buyer requirements.

Can you make charms for children’s products?

Yes. For charms that children will wear, we use phthalate-free soft PVC. Supplier material test reports are available on request, and finished-product testing such as CPSIA for the US can be arranged on your samples at an accredited lab, quoted separately.

Can I order custom shapes, colors and 3D designs?

We support flat, layered and raised soft-PVC designs. Send AI/PDF/SVG artwork or a clear reference image; we will review color count, minimum line width, raised layers and backing before confirming feasibility.

Will you sell or show our designs?

No. Your artwork is used only for your orders. We do not sell your designs to other buyers or show them anywhere without your permission.

Do you sell single pieces or dropship?

No. We sell to businesses only, from 100 pieces per design.

How do I request a quote and sample pricing?

Send the artwork, number of designs, quantity per design, size, intended market and delivery city/postal code. We will answer the budget questions first, then confirm the final mold, sample, production and shipping costs.
